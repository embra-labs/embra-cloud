Your data when contacting Embra

Updated 2026-10-07

1. Who receives your information?

Hoàng Xuân operates Embra and receives your requests directly. Contact: hello@embra.cloud.

2. What does this page collect?

The landing page has no signup form and does not create accounts. Clicking an email link opens your mail app; you choose what to send. When you contact us, your email address or Zalo account details and the content you send are used to discuss your project, setup and pricing.

3. Where does your information go?

Mail to and from hello@embra.cloud is handled through Gmail by Google LLC, so your email address and messages may be processed outside Vietnam. If you choose Zalo, the conversation takes place on Zalo’s systems. Do not include passwords, API keys or customer data in your initial project message.

4. How is your information used?

Information you send is used to respond to your request and discuss deployment. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for advertising. Service terms, project data locations and retention periods are agreed before setup.

5. Want to access, correct or delete your information?

Email hello@embra.cloud to request access, correction, a copy or deletion of information you have sent. You can also ask us to stop contacting you, restrict processing or object to it.

6. Previously joined the waitlist?

Previous registrations remain covered by the October 5, 2026 notice (Vietnamese) you agreed to. This landing-page update does not change that consent.
