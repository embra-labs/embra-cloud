Frequently asked questions

01How do I get started?

Email hello@embra.cloud or message us on Zalo with your stack and requirements. We will discuss configuration, pricing and setup.

02What if I only have a frontend?

Send us your landing page, portfolio or SPA. You do not need a backend or database to start the conversation.

03How much does it cost?

Quotes are based on resources and support needs. Configuration, pricing and service terms are agreed before deployment.

04Can I move an existing application?

Share your current setup, database size and downtime requirements. We will confirm suitability and a migration plan before you change your running system.

05Is restore always lossless?

Do not assume that. Restoring an earlier point can lose changes made after that point. Backup plans, recovery procedures and limits are discussed for each project.

06Are auth, email and queues included?

Additional services are not included by default. Share your requirements so we can confirm integrations and costs in your quote.

FROM THE FOUNDER

I once broke my company’s database.

When building Embra, I think about that situation: the app is down, people are waiting, and the person handling it has to work out what data was lost, whether the backup is usable and how long recovery will take.

In a small team, the person writing code often also deploys it and handles incidents. That is why I want risk reviews and recovery planning to be part of the setup conversation.

Where are you getting stuck? I would like to hear about your project and work out a suitable deployment with you.
